03 · how it works

Every session visits the memory three times.

  1. 01

    It starts by reading

    Who you are, your corrections, your open threads and your latest sessions are in front of the agent.

  2. 02

    It gets the relevant notes with every message

    Your message is searched in the memory; the few most relevant sections are added quietly.

  3. 03

    It ends by writing

    What was done, why, what is still open. The next session starts from there.
