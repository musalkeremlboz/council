One memory, every agent.

COUNCIL is an open-source system that gives AI agents a lasting memory.

To set it up, paste this sentence to your AI agent. It does the rest.

What it does

AI agents forget everything when the chat ends. COUNCIL fills that gap.

  • A new session does not start from zero

    As the session opens, your profile, your rules, your open threads and your latest sessions are in front of the agent.

  • Your correction becomes a rule

    Your correction becomes one dated line in the rules and loads in every later session.

  • All your devices share one memory

    Move it to a server and all your devices share the same notes; there is no folder to sync.

  • The memory is plain text

    Notes are plain text; every change stays in the history with when and why it was made.
